# Top Emerging Barriers: Wishlist to Purchase Conversion

Based on recent user sentiment, the primary friction points are no longer related to product selection or pricing, but rather **operational reliability** and **lack of communication**. These failures are causing immediate churn and severe brand damage.

*   **Chronic Order Processing Delays**: Users report orders stuck in pending status for up to a month despite multiple attempts. This erodes trust in the platform’s ability to fulfill basic transactions, causing users to abandon carts due to uncertainty.
*   **Flawed Last-Mile Logistics & False Status Updates**: A critical pattern of "Out for Delivery" status followed by "Unreachable" without actual contact attempts. This indicates systemic issues with carrier coordination or digital status automation, leading to frustration.
*   **Opaque Return/Cancellation Policies**: Users feel trapped in the system; products are marked as returning to the warehouse without notice or a clear cancellation pathway, removing user control and driving negative sentiment toward app UX.

## Recommended Action

1.  **Immediate Logistics Audit**: Partner with key 3PL providers to investigate the "unreachable" data discrepancy. Implement real-time driver tracking links that update only upon verified GPS proximity scans.
2.  **Proactive Communication Protocol**: Deploy automated SMS/WhatsApp alerts *before* an order is deemed unreachable, guiding the user to confirm availability. Never mark an order as "returned" without a final confirmation attempt from a human agent.
3.  **Transparency on Processing Times**: If backlogs exist, display accurate estimated processing windows on the cart page. If order delays exceed 48 hours, trigger an automated service recovery voucher to retain the customer’s trust.
